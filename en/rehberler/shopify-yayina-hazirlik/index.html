What should be checked before launching the Shopify store?

The opening of the store alone does not indicate that the order flow is ready. Check product information, mobile storefront, payment, shipping, and customer communication separately.

Muhammet Çay · 15 November 2026

Product and storefront check

Open the same product on both desktop and mobile. Are the product name, price, image, variant, and description consistent? If there are variants that cannot be selected or missing descriptions, add them to the pre-launch list. Check that products are accessible from collection menus and how out-of-stock products are displayed. You must hold the usage rights for brand images and the approval of the texts.

Business decisions

Payment accounts, shipping zones, fees, and business policy texts are not information to be decided by the developer alone. Shopify's official launch checklist handles store layout, business settings, and tests in separate steps. Verify the suitability of the payment method to be used for your country and business with the provider's current terms. This guide is not a tax or legal assessment.

Controlled test table

  • Product → cart: Are the correct variant and quantity being added?
  • Cart → checkout: Are address, shipping, and payment options in the expected format?
  • Failed transaction: Is what happened shown to the user in a clear manner?
  • Notification: Are order and support details going to the correct business?
  • Mobile: Are buttons, dropdown menus, and texts within the usable area?
  • Contact: Does the email or WhatsApp link open the correct destination?

Before creating a real order

Use the payment provider's test mode if available. To prevent tests from mixing with live transactions and creating false notifications, determine in advance who should be notified. A final check of the publishing settings is required when completing the test. Making a real payment or initiating a cancellation or refund process is a separate and explicit decision.

Post-launch responsibility

Decide who will manage product changes, stocks, and app subscriptions. Separate the delivered work from the ongoing operation of the business. The scope of the Shopify store editing service is determined accordingly. The Veronika review is an example of product, page, and Turkish interface editing.
