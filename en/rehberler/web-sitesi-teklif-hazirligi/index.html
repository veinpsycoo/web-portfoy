How to prepare a request for proposal before getting a website built?

To receive a comparable website quote, first list the pages, content responsibility, and the actions the visitor will take. A design example alone does not explain the scope of work.

Muhammet Çay · 15 November 2026

1. Select the single main action the visitor will take

For a service business, the goal may be a phone call or a WhatsApp conversation; for a store, it may be a purchase, or for an educational product, a registration. Instead of writing "a modern site," write "the customer should understand my service and request a quote via this channel." This decision determines the content order and the contact button on the homepage. If there are multiple goals, specify which one comes first.

2. Count the content and function, not the page

Homepage, services, portfolio, and contact are a starting list. Add who will prepare the content for each page. Simply promoting products is not the same scope as stock, payment, and order management. Create separate lines if there is membership, multi-language support, booking, and an admin panel. Also list the addresses of existing pages; the loss of valuable URLs must be prevented while the site is being renewed.

3. Copyable preparation list

  • Business and target customer: Who am I serving, and for which need?
  • Main action: What should the visitor do on the site?
  • Content: Which texts, logos, and licensed images are ready?
  • Function: Are forms, payments, appointments, accounts, or external system connections required?
  • Management: After launch, who will update what?
  • Existing site: Which URLs must be preserved and which contents will be migrated?
  • Delivery: What is the scope for domain, hosting, source files, maintenance, and revisions?

4. Compare offers with the same scope

Comparing only the total amount is misleading. One offer may include product entry and mobile testing, while another may only include theme installation. Included and excluded items, content quantity, paid services, maintenance responsibility, and delivery format must be in writing. If you are required to perform updates, the necessary interface and usage instructions must also be included in the delivery.

When are you ready to meet?

Not every detail needs to be ready. A sample customer flow and the parts of the above items that you are aware of will make the first meeting more concrete. Review the website service scope and the implementation example of this portfolio. This list is not a price tariff; it is prepared to make the differences in scope within the proposals visible.
