VIDEO & MOTION

Video projects for products, brands, and social media.

Commercials, product promotions, animation, and 3D works. Select a category, turn on the sound, and watch the examples.

How does a video project begin?

We start by discussing your product, your target audience, and where the video will be used. The same content may require a detailed explanation on a product page, while needing a shorter flow for a vertical social media video. The examples below show works of different types; the scope of the new project is clarified separately.

Scope and deliverables

  • The agreed-upon narrative, scene flow, and editing.
  • Preparation of product images or videos for which you provide usage rights; AI-supported visual production if necessary.
  • Subtitles, audio editing, and format suitable for the publishing channel according to the scope.
  • Approved output files; determination of different sizes and revision scope during the proposal stage.

What should you prepare for the meeting?

Share a brief description of the product or service, the visuals you have, any existing videos, the publishing channel, and examples you like. Do not send personal data or customer images without sharing permission. If there is a need for music, stock content, voiceover, or filming, rights and additional costs will be evaluated separately.

Factors affecting price and duration

The number of scenes as well as video length, the quality of source material, production method, editing intensity, and requested formats are decisive. On-site filming, actors, media buying, and ad account management are not automatically included in this service. No guarantee of views, sales, or advertising return is provided.
