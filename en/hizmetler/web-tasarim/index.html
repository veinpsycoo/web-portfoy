Services

Website design and development

I develop websites that describe your business, are mobile-friendly, and guide visitors toward contact. For a new site or the renewal of your existing site, we first clarify the scope of content and functionality.

Tell us your needs ↗

Who is it suitable for?

For businesses that want to describe their services online, companies that sell their products via quotes, and personal brands. The primary goal is for the visitor to get to know you, see your work, and reach you through the correct channel. For a store where sales will be completed directly on the site, you can take a look at the Shopify store project.

What is prepared?

  • Page and menu plan; home page, service or product introduction, about us, and contact areas.
  • Desktop and mobile interface suitable for your brand; placement of text, visuals, and links.
  • Basic publishing settings such as titles, page descriptions, sharing images, canonicals, and sitemaps.
  • Publishing on the agreed communication links and domain; necessary usage notes for takeover.

How does the process work?

First, we discuss your existing site, your customers, and the action you want the visitor to take. Once the content list and page plan are clarified, we proceed via a design preview. The approved scope is implemented; it is published after checking the mobile view, menus, links, and communication channels. If there is a need for manageable content, the panel selection is made before this stage.

What is required to start?

A business and service description, logo, images you hold the publishing rights to, sites you take as examples, and current domain information are a sufficient start. You do not need to send passwords during the first meeting; necessary accesses are planned securely once the scope of the work is clarified. The Website proposal preparation guide helps you identify your missing elements.

What changes the price and scope?

Along with the number of pages, content preparation, number of languages, custom interactions, management needs, and connections with external systems are the determining factors. Whether copywriting, photography, hosting, domain, paid plugins, and ongoing maintenance are included is specified separately in the proposal. An appointment, payment, or membership system is not the same task as a standard promotional site. I do not provide a fixed price or delivery date without seeing the scope.
