Services

Shopify store setup and organization

I prepare an organized Shopify store with products, collections, and brand pages. For new store setup or the renewal of an existing storefront, the delivery scope is determined according to your product and content needs.

Tell us your needs ↗

Which needs is this suitable for?

For brands wanting to sell their products online and businesses using Shopify but wanting to organize their storefront. In stores where product pages are disconnected, menus are cluttered, or mobile usage is difficult, the existing structure is first analyzed.

Scope of work

  • Customization of brand colors, typography, and store sections via the theme.
  • Preparation of the agreed number of products, variants, and collections.
  • Placement of About Us, Contact, and policy texts provided by the client into the appropriate areas.
  • Turkish interface texts and mobile storefront controls; auditing of product, cart, and contact links.

Payment, shipping, and legal texts

Payment provider acceptance, bank accounts, tax, and shipping agreements are the business's processes. The provider, application, and links to be used are checked separately before the proposal. Legal text production or legal compliance guarantee is not part of this service; texts approved by the business are used. Paid themes, applications, and Shopify subscriptions are separate expenses.

How do we proceed?

First, the product list, images, variants, and the target market are clarified. A storefront draft is prepared with the existing theme or a suitable theme option. Store checks are performed after product placement and content approval. If payment and shipping are included, a suitable testing method is determined together without creating a real customer order. Maintenance and product entry responsibilities after launch are decided separately.

Prepare for a proposal

Product names, descriptions, prices, and images; variant structure; logo; existing store link, and the changes you desire. The number of products, data cleaning, number of languages, theme customization, and integrations affect the cost and duration. You can use the pre-launch store checklist.

Portfolio proof

The Veronika Shopify project is an example of product entry, page layout, and Turkish interface work. The project review explains the applied scope; it does not include claims of sales increases or commercial results.
