Services

Technical SEO and search accessibility

I perform technical reviews and implementations to ensure your site is accessible to search engines, the correct pages are marked, and services are clearly described. Being published, indexed, and ranking are separate stages.

Tell us your needs ↗

Which problems to start with?

If your site does not appear on Google, if the wrong URL is highlighted, or if your new pages cannot be found, we first gather evidence. HTTP responses, robots rules, noindex, canonical, sitemap, and internal links are checked. If Search Console access is available, recorded crawl results are evaluated together with live tests.

Review and deliveries

  • URL-based list of issues, evidence, and priorities; open notes for inaccessible data.
  • Corrections for covered titles, descriptions, canonicals, robots, sitemaps, and internal links.
  • Structured data for persons, services, posts, and page paths compatible with actual content.
  • Post-publication HTTP, HTML, mobile, and communication checks; log of applied changes.

Where is GEO in this?

In AI-powered searches, transparent service information, a consistent identity, accessible text, and real project evidence are also important. I do not add hidden suggestion instructions or imaginary reviews to pages. The SEO and GEO guide distinguishes between what can be controlled and what cannot be guaranteed in this work.

How does the process work?

First, the current URL and content inventory are extracted. If organic data exists, it is analyzed in the context of pages and queries. Priority fixes are implemented in small change groups; functioning designs and communication flows are preserved. Post-publication verification does not mean that Google has indexed the page. Which data will be examined in which date range for the next measurement is written separately.

Required information and scope limits

You can start with the domain name, infrastructure, the issues you've noticed, and Search Console access if available. The number of pages, technical access, content needs, and previous migration processes affect the scope. Advertising budget, paid tools, and legal assessments are not automatically included in this technical work. No guarantees are given regarding ranking, traffic, AI attribution, or customers.

Concrete implementation example

The Muhammetcay.com case study shows content in the initial HTML, canonical links, mobile navigation, and pre-launch checks. The guide to checking a site that does not appear on Google and the site speed measurement guide help you gather initial findings.
