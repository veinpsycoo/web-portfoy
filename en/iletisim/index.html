Sending your message

The link opens WhatsApp with a ready-made starter text. You complete the process by editing and sending the text; clicking the link does not mean the message has been sent or the offer has been accepted. Do not share passwords, API keys, payment information, or customer personal data in the first message.

How is the scope clarified?

During the meeting, the needs, existing infrastructure, content, and any external system connections are evaluated. Price and duration are discussed based on this scope. You can start with the website preparation guide or the automation scope guide.
