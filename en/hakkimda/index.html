About Muhammet Çay

I work on websites, software, games, and visual content. Here you can find both my own projects and the work I have undertaken for my clients, categorized by their roles.

Design, software, and content at the same table

I am Muhammet Çay; I use the name Veinpsyco in the digital space. I prepare websites and stores for businesses, work on custom software and automation, and develop game and visual content projects. Alongside client work, I manage my own web products, e-commerce stores, and content sites.

I start by defining the problem and the output to be used. Page design, product entry, data flow, editing, or publication preparation: I specify the scope of the work I do for each project separately.
